## The 5 most influential sources
1. **Google Email sender guidelines FAQ** (https://support.google.com/a/answer/14229414) — the canonical numeric rulebook: 5,000/day bulk definition, spam rate < 0.1% (ceiling 0.3%), SPF+DKIM+DMARC+alignment, RFC 8058 one-click, PTR, TLS, RFC 5322, Nov-2025 full enforcement. weapon-forge: this is the spine of the deliverability preflight; encode every figure verbatim.
2. **Microsoft "Strengthening Email Ecosystem" + Validity explainer** — May 5, 2025 enforcement, the `550 5.7.515` reject code, junk-then-reject disposition. weapon-forge: this is the "why deliverability gates everything / the suspension story" evidence; surface the reject code in the no-go verdict.
3. **Yahoo Sender Hub best practices** (https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/) — DKIM ≥1024-bit, DMARC p=none + relaxed alignment, spam < 0.3%, RFC 8058, CFL enrollment. weapon-forge: the third leg of the three-provider preflight table.
4. **GoHighLevel official Email Sending Guide + DMARC article** — the primary-platform runbook: dedicated subdomain, phased warmup (100/hr→1k/day, 300/hr→2.5k/day, ~4wk), and the `p=reject`-without-dedicated-domain bounce footgun. weapon-forge: build the GHL setup runbook and the DMARC footgun check as first-class.
5. **Google Postmaster Tools dashboards + Mailtrap 2026 tutorial** — Spam Rate + Compliance Status are the v2 panels; reputation dashboards were REMOVED; verify via DKIM domain. weapon-forge: encode the enrollment gate (Postmaster + SNDS/JMRP + Yahoo CFL) — this exact gap killed the first client's campaign.

## Gaps weapon-forge should be aware of
- **Cold-email tracking/redirect infrastructure** was only lightly covered (one search returned thin results on vanity redirect domains). The Weapon's cold-send link-hygiene rule (no shorteners, consistent tracking domain) is grounded, but the deeper "custom tracking subdomain reputation" topic could use a targeted follow-up if cold outreach becomes a heavy use case.
- **Primary-source depth on Microsoft** is corroborated via vendor (Validity) because the official Tech Community blog body did not extract via WebFetch. The figures (5k/day, May 5 2025, 550 5.7.515, p=none minimum) are consistent across multiple sources but a Firecrawl re-fetch of the official page would let weapon-forge quote Microsoft directly.
- **M3AAWG primary documents** (named in the brief's reference list) were not fetched directly; the best-practices they codify are reflected through the provider docs and vendor guides. A targeted M3AAWG pull is a candidate for an extreme-tier follow-up if the Weapon needs to cite the standards body verbatim.
- **CAN-SPAM / GDPR statutory text** was treated as a known directive from the brief (working unsubscribe, physical address, honest From/Reply-To) rather than re-researched; weapon-forge should encode these as compliance rules but they were not independently sourced in this sweep.
- **Numbers drift fast.** Spam-rate thresholds and enforcement dates are current as of 2026-06-25. The Weapon should date-stamp these and flag them for re-verification each quarter.
