# Mixpost: self-hosted social media management (one-time license)

## Summary
Mixpost (inovector/mixpost) is the other self-hosted option in the arsenal's selection table. Privacy-first, one-time-payment licensing (no subscription), with a REST API + webhooks. Supplies the licensing model and platform coverage to complete the self-hosted comparison.

## Key quotations / statistics
- Pricing: Pro license $299 ONE-TIME (no recurring), unlimited users, 14-day money-back. Lite package is free and open-source; advanced features need Pro/Enterprise.
- Updates: Pro/Enterprise include 1 year of free updates; software keeps working after, updates require renewal.
- API: "RESTful API and webhooks that allow for programmatic content creation, media uploads, and account management," plus custom integrations with CRMs/dashboards.
- Platforms (11, Pro/Enterprise): Facebook, Instagram (posts/reels/stories), Threads, X, LinkedIn, YouTube (videos/shorts), TikTok, Pinterest, Mastodon, Bluesky, Google Business Profile.
- GitHub: inovector/mixpost. Tagline: "Schedule, publish, and manage on your server. No subscriptions, no limits. (Buffer alternative)."
