# Financing media contract pointer

The private photo-led financing increment consumes [CTR-001, revision 1](../../../knowledge/private/contracts/CTR-001-financing-property-media.md), accepted by the orchestrator on October 13, 2026 as an engineering agreement under the owner's delegated project authoring authority.

This is the shared producer/consumer contract for tenant-private photograph intake, frozen financing v2 media/facts and authenticated HTML/PDF/report outputs. Its exact typed ports, bounds, retry behavior, ownership and v1 compatibility govern the implementation tracked in [the financing photo ledger](../../../../FINANCING_PHOTOS_LEDGER.md).

Contract acceptance does not implement a product criterion or approve lender content, public publication, live storage, provider operation or visual output. The full [payment/report/site requirements](payment-flyers-and-co-branded-sites.md) and the original [text-only financing increment](financing-report-increment.md) retain their distinct scopes and evidence.
