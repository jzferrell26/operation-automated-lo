# JTBD Interview Script: Founding Cohort Demand

**Opportunity being explored:** The job of turning one Realtor property into a connected campaign and measurable HighLevel lead path.

**Participant:** Loan officer or mortgage marketing operator with recent property-campaign experience.

**Length:** 20 to 30 minutes.

**Cadence:** At least one interview each week. Seek five to seven interviews in the same cluster before claiming saturation.

## Pre-Interview

- Confirm the participant fits the target segment.
- Request recording consent separately. No recording without affirmative consent.
- Do not request borrower data, provider credentials, card data, or client account screenshots containing PII.
- Keep the product pitch out of Acts 1 through 4.
- Book a 20-minute debrief immediately after the call.

## Act 1: Context, 3 to 5 Minutes

- Tell me about your role and the kinds of property or Realtor campaigns you support.
- How often do you run an open house or property campaign?
- Which parts of the work are yours, and which parts belong to a Realtor, assistant, compliance team, or agency?

## Act 2: Current Workflow, 3 to 5 Minutes

- Walk me through how you handle a property campaign today.
- What does a good campaign week look like? What makes one go badly?
- Which systems or people have to stay coordinated?

Do not mention Operation Automated LO yet.

## Act 3: Specific Recent Story, 10 to 15 Minutes

- Think about the last property or open house campaign you worked on. Start at the moment you first knew the campaign had to happen. What happened next?
- What made the campaign important at that moment?
- What did you do for the page, flyer or PDF, QR destination, ad creative, follow-up, and CRM tracking?
- Where did you wait, repeat work, copy information, or ask someone else for help?
- What compliance, approval, or targeting concern changed what you did?
- How did you know whether the campaign produced a useful lead, appointment, application, or funded outcome?
- What did the process cost in time, outside help, software, or missed opportunity?
- What have you already tried to improve it?

### Four Forces Notes

| Force | Evidence to capture |
| --- | --- |
| Push | Specific frustration or failure in the prior workflow |
| Pull | Outcome that made a different approach attractive |
| Anxiety | Risk, compliance, setup, trust, or change concern |
| Habit | Existing process or relationship that creates inertia |

## Act 4: Decision Model, 5 Minutes

- When you consider changing this workflow, what matters most?
- What would a new approach have to prove before you trusted it?
- Which parts must remain under human approval?
- What would make you reject a product even if the demo looked good?
- What does good enough look like for the first campaign?
- Who else must agree before you can pay for or install a product like this?

## Act 5: Close, 2 to 3 Minutes

- Is there anything important about the last campaign that I did not ask?
- If you could change one part of the current process, what would it be?
- May we contact you once for clarification or a future evidence review?

Thank the participant and end the research interview before any commercial invitation.

## Optional Commercial Invitation After the Interview

Only after the interview is explicitly closed may the interviewer ask whether the participant wants to receive the separate founding demo invitation. A yes records permission to send the invitation, not demand validation. Do not ask whether they would hypothetically buy during the research interview.

## Debrief Record

Capture outside Git in the authorized research system:

- Participant pseudonymous ID
- Interview date and consent state
- Specific recent event
- Direct quotes with PII removed
- Push, pull, anxiety, and habit evidence
- Candidate opportunity statements in participant language
- Buying authority and eligibility signals
- Custom-service requests
- Follow-up permission

Update the OST only from completed evidence. Do not add a node because it fits the product thesis.
