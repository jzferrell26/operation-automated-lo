# Operation Automated LO Discovery

This folder contains the evidence plan for the G8 paid-founder demand gate.

The package does not authorize production implementation and does not claim that G8 is closed. The working product demo may contain synthetic, manual, and unavailable states, and those boundaries must be stated before any payment decision.

## Guardrails

- Completed payment is the demand signal. Verbal interest, demo attendance, and checkout starts are supporting signals only.
- At least 15 eligible, distinct founder accounts must remain paid under the approved offer terms before G8 can pass.
- Under 10 paid accounts after the complete experiment means reshape or stop.
- A result of 10 to 14 does not authorize production. It requires a documented decision review.
- Participant PII, payment-card data, provider credentials, borrower information, and raw recordings never enter Git.
- No email, checkout, charge, provider call, or live-account change is performed by this documentation package.
