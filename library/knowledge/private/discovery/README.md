# Operation Automated LO Discovery

This folder contains the evidence plan for the G8 paid-founder demand criterion.

On 2026-07-20, the product owner directed the team to proceed without evidence of 15 paid founders. G8 is therefore recorded as **ACCEPTED CONSTRAINT**, never PASS. No evidence currently proves the 15-paid-founder threshold, so commercial validation remains unproven.

This package neither fabricates demand evidence nor claims that G8 passed. The working product demo may contain synthetic, manual, and unavailable states, and those boundaries must be stated before any later payment decision. The paid-demand experiment remains available as post-start learning.

## Guardrails

- Completed payment is the demand signal. Verbal interest, demo attendance, and checkout starts are supporting signals only.
- At least 15 eligible, distinct founder accounts must remain paid under the approved offer terms before the demand criterion could qualify for an independent PASS review.
- The 15-paid-founder threshold is not an implementation prerequisite under the 2026-07-20 product-owner decision.
- This decision changes only G8. G1 through G7 remain separate and unchanged.
- Under 10 paid accounts after the complete experiment means reshape or stop.
- A result of 10 to 14 is commercially inconclusive and requires a documented learning review.
- Participant PII, payment-card data, provider credentials, borrower information, and raw recordings never enter Git.
- No email, checkout, charge, provider call, or live-account change is performed by this documentation package.
