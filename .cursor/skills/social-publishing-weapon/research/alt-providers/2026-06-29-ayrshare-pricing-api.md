# Ayrshare: pricing, platforms, and API model

## Summary
Ayrshare is the "category incumbent" unified social API. The arsenal weapon names it as an extensible alternative but does not detail it. This source supplies its pricing tiers, profile model, and posting capability so weapon-forge can build a real comparison row.

## Key quotations / statistics
- Post model: create a post with text, images, or video and "send it immediately or schedule it for a future date or time"; delete by post id to remove from all networks.
- Platforms: "13+ social platforms" (incl. Reddit, Pinterest, Snapchat, Telegram, YouTube).
- Pricing tiers (from pricing page, 2026):
  - Premium: $149/mo ($1,548/yr) - 1 Social Profile/Brand, 13+ platforms, schedule unlimited posts, basic analytics, API access.
  - Launch: $299/mo (28-day free trial) - up to 10 Profiles, advanced analytics, expanded API rate limits, custom branding, WEBHOOK integrations.
  - Business: $599/mo ($5,988/yr) - 30 Profiles included, scales to 5000, unlimited team members.
  - Enterprise: Custom - thousands of profiles, real-time X/Twitter webhooks, advanced security/compliance.
- Free tier: ambiguous across sources. Pricing page (fetched) shows "no free tier" with Premium as entry; search snippets cite a $0 plan (1 profile, 50 posts/mo, images only). FLAG: free-tier availability changed in 2026; the paid entry point is $149/mo. The pay-per-active-profile model means cost scales with profiles used.

## Annotations for weapon-forge
- SELECTION-TABLE INPUT: Ayrshare is the mature, expensive option. Entry is steep ($149/mo for ONE profile). For an agency with many client accounts, cost scales hard - this is the "expensive at scale" knock other vendors make, and it is grounded in the per-profile pricing.
- Ayrshare DOES support immediate vs scheduled posting and delete-by-id, similar surface to GHL/Zernio. It supports webhooks from the Launch tier up.
- PUBLISH-GATE NOTE: Ayrshare's documented states are "immediate" or "scheduled (future date)." Like GHL/Zernio, a scheduled post auto-publishes at its time; weapon-forge should carry the same drafts-only caution and verify whether Ayrshare exposes a true draft/approval hold.
- CONTRADICTION across sources on the free tier - weapon-forge should treat $149/mo as the reliable paid entry and mark the free tier as "verify current."
