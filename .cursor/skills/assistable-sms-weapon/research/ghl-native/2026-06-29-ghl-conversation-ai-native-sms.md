# GoHighLevel native Conversation AI on SMS (2026)

## Summary

GHL's native Conversation AI is the strategic "replace-Assistable" target's nearest off-the-shelf competitor and the platform the cuantico-sms migration sits beside. It is a trained chatbot deployed across SMS, live chat, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, and WhatsApp; enabling it on the SMS channel lets it auto-reply to inbound texts using a configured knowledge base to answer questions, qualify leads, and book appointments. The strategic argument for going native (whether GHL-native or a custom GHL-native engine like cuantico-sms) is data proximity: the AI has direct access to contacts, calendar, pipeline, and workflows without third-party "zaps."

## Key quotations / statistics

- "Unlike third-party chatbot tools you duct-tape onto your CRM, GHL's AI is native -- it has access to your contacts, your calendar, your pipeline, and your workflows."
- "GoHighLevel reduced latency on AI-generated replies by roughly 40% compared to the Q4 2025 baseline ... contacts in SMS and live chat conversations are getting responses in under two seconds on average."
- "The bot now retains full conversation history across multiple sessions rather than treating each new message thread as a fresh interaction."
- Pricing: "for a flat fee of $97/month per sub-account, you get the AI Employee add-on, which includes unlimited usage of Conversation AI, Voice AI, and all related features."
- Enable path: "Settings -> AI Employee ... enable the feature and select which channels you want the AI to manage: SMS, web chat, email, or voice."
- "For 90% of local businesses and agencies, the native GHL Conversation AI is more than enough ... Only 'Power Users' with highly complex, multi-service sales funnels truly need a custom external bot."
