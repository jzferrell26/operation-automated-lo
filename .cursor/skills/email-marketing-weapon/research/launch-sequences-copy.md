## Launch cadence (warm broadcast — the 6-12 email runway)
- Sweet spot: **7-12 emails across a 7-14 day window.** Send roughly every other day in the main launch, then **2-3 emails in the final 48 hours** — over 50% of buyers purchase at the last minute, so back-load the cadence.
- A simple event spine: **Invite → Reminder → Starting now → Thank you + replay.**
- Webinar registration math (set client expectations):
  - A single invitation captures **40-50%** of total registrations at best.
  - A follow-up to non-registrants with a DIFFERENT subject/angle captures another **20-30%.**
  - Send **2-3 invitations with varied angles** — outcome-focused, speaker-focused, urgency-focused.

## Webinar email spine (per-email roles)
1. **Invitation** — topic, compelling brief description, link to registration landing page, strong single CTA.
2. **Day-before reminder** — rekindle interest, confirm details.
3. **One-hour-before reminder** — prompt to wrap up and prep.
4. **"We're live" / starting-now** — sent at exact start time; a direct one-link "we're live" email can lift live attendance **10-15%.**
5. **Post-webinar follow-up + replay** — send the replay within **2-4 hours** of ending; same-day follow-ups get **2-3x** the engagement of next-day.
- Post-webinar/launch close: stack value → objection-handling → urgency/scarcity → final-call (the 2-3 emails in the last 48h).

## Subject lines + preheaders
- Length: **30-50 characters**, specific, clear about what's inside. **Clarity beats cleverness.**
- Subject + preheader = a **two-line headline**: subject grabs, preheader deepens the hook with context the subject couldn't fit. **Write at least ~75 characters of preheader** so it isn't truncated or auto-filled with body text.
- Proven structures (from sources):
  - Status/time markers: "STARTING SOON • [topic]", "Starting NOW • [topic]", "See you tomorrow • [topic]".
  - Curiosity + date: "Something's coming Thursday (you'll want to clear your calendar)".
  - Outcome-framed invite: "You're invited: [specific outcome]".
- Reframe: "Nobody registers for webinars — they register for knowledge, skills, and results." Lead with the transformation; the word "webinar" is secondary.

## CTA discipline
- One primary CTA per email. The reminder/live emails should be a single direct link. Repeat the one CTA rather than offering competing actions.

## Re-engagement / sunset copy (cross-ref monitoring-warmup-hygiene.md)
- 2-3 re-engagement emails over 4-6 weeks before suppression; use click-based engagement, a clear "do you still want to hear from us?" ask, then suppress non-responders.
