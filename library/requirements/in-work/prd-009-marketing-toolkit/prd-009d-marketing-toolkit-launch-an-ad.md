# PRD-009d: Marketing Toolkit - Launch an Ad

> **Parent:** [PRD-009](./prd-009-marketing-toolkit-index.md)
> **Status:** In Work (Gauntlet run started 2026-10-01). Authored 2026-10-01 after OD-H. It replaces the three-step open house launch of the first draft (commit `22e6b87`).
> **Priority:** P0. This is the product's core: "They select the one they want and launch. Nothing crazy." (OD-H)
> **Schema changes:** None in the database. It uses 009c's manifest variant and adds rules to the domain checks.
> **Owner Guardians:** `react-guardian` (the flow, the brand band, the Brand page fields); `typescript-node-guardian` (the save, the checks); `meta-ads-guardian` (verifies Meta's current Special Ad Category rules during the run, 009D-AC-012)

## Goal

"Launch an ad" in three steps at `/marketing/campaigns/new`:

1. **Choose an ad** from the library.
2. **Set it up:** the loan officer's brand goes on the ad automatically; only the headline and primary text can change; the disclosure is locked; budget, dates, and where it shows are set within Meta's Special Ad Category rules.
3. **Review and launch:** the actual ad at its real shape, what the approval covers, "Approve this version" with its own confirmation, and "Launch on Facebook", which stays disabled with one honest sentence.

Every PRD-008b truthfulness state holds on every surface, and the happy path meets the design's click and field targets.

Nothing is published or sent." at `:170`).
2. **The server save** (`apps/web/src/server/open-house-draft.ts`) mints a new campaign reference every time (`:76-79`), records constant profile references (`:90-97`), targets `country: "US"` and one typed region (`:146-152`), checks against banned phrases "guaranteed approval" and "no credit check" (`:175`), and takes the disclosure and the lead-form consent straight from the request body (`:30-31`, `:112-113`). ## Non-Goals

- Publishing anything: no launch route, no Meta or HighLevel call, no inline launch confirmation, no publishing progress, no raster image. Those belong to the future Meta publish PRD.
- Editing the image or layout of a library ad (OD-H: "Just the copy").
- A logo upload. The band uses an initials tile (D-25); a logo needs file storage and is an owner question for later.
- Any Realtor identity on an ad. Compliance control 9 stays in force; the flow reads no Realtor partner data.
- Instagram (D-23), radius targeting, ZIP codes, age, gender, interests, or audiences (D-18).

## Design decisions

### D1. Steps, addresses, and what is saved when

The step is in the address (`?step=1`, `2`, or `3`); `?topic=` opens step 1 filtered and `?ad=<id>` opens step 2 with that ad chosen. Each value is typed: `step` is an integer from 1 to 3, `topic` one of the five topic ids, `ad` an id the server resolves from the catalog, and `from` (where "Cancel" returns) one of `home`, `campaigns`, or `library`. Anything else is ignored, and no address value is ever used as a URL, so "Cancel" cannot be turned into an open redirect. Nothing is saved until "Save and check" on step 2, which saves the version and runs the checks. Step 3's address carries the saved campaign reference, so a reload re-reads it. A reload on step 2 keeps the chosen ad (from the address) and its prefills. Back moves between steps without losing typed words.

### D2. Step 1 is the library

Step 1 shows the ad cards, the topic chips with counts, and the card grid inside the step indicator. 009d builds those three components (Wave 2); 009c's library tab reuses them in Wave 3. "Use this ad" goes straight to step 2; there is no separate Continue. On this choosing screen every card button is secondary, so no single blue button competes with the ads (design section 6.1); "Cancel" returns to the page `?from` names, or to Campaigns when it names none (D1).

### D3. The brand band, and three copy corrections

The band follows `design/00-direction.md` section 5.4: the bottom 270 px of the tall ad and the bottom 22 percent of the square ad; white with navy text; an initials tile (D-25) and the thin rule above the band in the brand colour (D-24); the name in bold, then title and NMLS number; the company and company NMLS number; the disclosure line along the bottom; a placeholder band ("Your name and NMLS number go here") when the person has no brand; a long name wraps to two lines, then shrinks to a 14 px floor (the secondary text step), then truncates.

Brand gains four fields, saved beside the report brand under the preference key `workspace.ad_brand.v1` (which satisfies the key check `^[a-z][a-z0-9_.]{1,63}$`, `supabase/migrations/20260919160000_user_preferences.sql:43`), not inside the homeowner report contract, so homeowner reports are unaffected:

- **Title** (optional, at most 60 characters);
- **Brand colour**, stored as a preset id from a constant table of six (the initials tile's letters pass 4.5:1 on each), refused otherwise, and mapped to a colour value only when the band renders; _(Amended 2026-10-02 by the PRD-009 writing review pass 2 (MTK-008, W-14): the field's label is "Brand color", because every other string in the product uses American spelling.)_
- **Disclosure line** (at most 120 characters, default "Equal Housing Opportunity.");
- **Lead form wording** (at most 300 characters). Its default is a new property-free sentence, "By submitting, you agree to be contacted about home financing and related mortgage services.", because today's starter consent text says "about this property" (`apps/web/src/features/guided-setup/model/profile.ts:53-55`), which is untrue for a library ad.

The save reads all of the band's brand values, the disclosure line, and the lead form wording on the server from the signed-in person's saved Brand under their tenant context. The request carries none of them. Every text field the band prints (name, title, company, disclosure line) and the lead form wording pass the same checks as the edited words (D5), and each finding names the Brand field to fix. The disclosure line is the person's lender-approved text (`compliance-and-risk.md:50` asks for lender-maintained disclosure blocks); counsel's review of that model is on the operator checklist.

Because PRD-009 has no logo upload, three design strings that promise a logo are corrected so the product never says something it cannot do:

| Where | Design string | PRD-009 string |
|---|---|---|
| Home lead | "Your name, NMLS number and logo go on it for you." | "Your name and NMLS number go on it for you." |
| Checklist brand item | "Your name, NMLS number and logo. They go on every ad automatically." | "Your name and NMLS number. They go on every ad automatically." |
| Library lead | "Your name, NMLS number and logo go on each one automatically." | "Your name and NMLS number go on each one automatically." |

### D4. Where it shows (D-18, D-23)

Places, not people: one or more cities or states, chosen in a structured field and shown as chips each with a 44 px remove button, and matched to Meta's locations only once Meta is connected (future work). Each value is validated, not stored as free text:

- **A state** is one of the 50 states or the District of Columbia, entered by full name or two-letter code and stored as the code.
- **A city** matches `^[A-Za-z][A-Za-z .'-]{1,59}, [A-Z]{2}$` with a listed state code ("Austin, TX").
- **No value** contains a digit, or any of the whole words (delimited by non-letters) zip, radius, mile, miles, within, age, male, female, men, or women, so "Page, AZ" and "Mendota Heights, MN" pass.
- **At most** 5 states and 10 cities.

_(Amended 2026-10-02 by the PRD-009 pre-redraw verification (W-1, W-2): the words refused in a city's name are wider than the list above. They are the audience words in `AD_PLACE_AUDIENCE_WORDS` (99: zip, postal, radius and within, and the age, gender, family and status words such as senior, women, gay, single, family and veteran), the distance units in `AD_PLACE_DISTANCE_UNITS` (mi, mile, miles, km, kms, kilometer, kilometers, kilometre, kilometres), and the number words in `AD_PLACE_NUMBER_WORDS` (one to twenty, thirty to ninety, hundred, thousand, dozen, few, several), all in `packages/contracts/src/ad-places.ts`, with the same lists in the domain held equal by a unit test. A word is matched against the name only, never the state code. A unit is refused only where it reads as a distance: beside a digit, beside a number word ("ten miles around Austin", "five mi from Dallas"; beside the singular "mile" only "one", because real names such as Nine Mile Falls, WA hold a number word and "Mile"), after "within", or as the last word of the name, with or without a full stop. A real place whose name holds a refused word passes only as the exact "Name, ST" pair in `AD_PLACE_NAMED_EXCEPTIONS` (33 pairs, each found in the USGS Geographic Names Information System on 2026-10-02): "Gay, GA" and "Miles, WA" pass, "Gay, TX" and "gay neighborhoods in Austin, TX" do not. A pair is added only after it is found there. 009D-AC-008 is unchanged and the total stays 120.)_

The request schema refuses anything else with 400, and the library-ad ruleset extends `TARGETING_NOT_ALLOWED` to refuse any stored state or city that fails these rules, so a ZIP code, a radius, or a demographic can never reach a saved version. Step 3's "What you approve" lists each city as "<City, ST> and everything within 15 miles" ("Austin, TX and everything within 15 miles") and each state by its full name ("Texas"), because Meta requires the area around a selected city to include everything within 15 miles ([Meta rules check](research/2026-10-02-meta-special-ad-category-check.md), section 3; `compliance-and-risk.md:72`). The first time the field is empty; afterwards the person's last area is prefilled. "Shows in: the Facebook feed." There is no control for radius, ZIP code, age, gender, interests, or audiences. The manifest records country `US`, the states as regions, the cities, and `placements: ["facebook_feed"]`, and the existing `TARGETING_NOT_ALLOWED` rule still applies.

The hint is "Mortgage ads can't be aimed by age, gender or ZIP code, so you choose places, not people. Around a city, Meta requires the area to include everything within 15 miles." Both Meta clauses are VERIFIED in the [Meta rules check](research/2026-10-02-meta-special-ad-category-check.md) (sections 3 and 4), as 009D-AC-012 requires before a clause ships. The design's longer hint also said Meta widens a small area; that clause does not ship, because Meta states a minimum area the advertiser must include, and automatic widening is UNVERIFIED.

### D5. Checks on the words, the brand, and the dates

The library-ad ruleset keeps every shared rule (disclosure, consent, images, banned phrases, claims, financing terms, housing category, targeting, budget, routing) and adds:

| Rule | Blocks when | Plain fix |
|---|---|---|
| `WORDS_TOO_LONG` | the headline or primary text exceeds the ad's `editable` limit | "Shorten the headline to 60 characters or fewer." (or the text's limit) |
| `WORDS_RATE_PAYMENT_OR_TERM_CLAIM` | the words state a rate, APR, payment, down payment amount, loan term, or a teaser such as "low rates" (`compliance-and-risk.md:48`) | "Take 'low rates' out of the headline. Ads can't state rate claims." |
| `WORDS_INVALID_CHARACTERS` | any checked text contains a Unicode control or format character (including U+200B to U+200F, U+202A to U+202E, U+2060 to U+2069, and U+FEFF), the headline contains a line break, or any checked text contains `<` or `>` | "Take out the hidden or special characters in the headline." (naming the field) |
| `WORDS_NUMBER` | any checked text contains `%` or `$`, or a number word next to percent, year, month, or payment ("three percent"); or the headline, primary text, title, or lead form wording contains a digit; or the name, company, or disclosure line contains a digit outside a license reference or, in the name and company only, an ordinal (below) | "Take the number out of the <field>. Ads can't state rates, payments or terms." |
| `WORDS_CO_BRAND` | any checked text contains a co-brand term: realtor, brokerage, broker (with the one phrase exception below), real estate agent, listed by, listing agent, in partnership with, presented by, courtesy of, sponsored by, the registered-mark symbol, an at sign, a web address (with the one host exception below), or a phone number; or a saved Realtor partner's name or company of at least 4 characters after normalising | "Take <term> out of the ad text. Paid ads show only you." (naming the field) |
| `WORDS_PRIVATE_INFO_REQUEST` | any checked text names a private detail a person could be asked to type in (the private-details list below) | "Take <term> out of the <field>. Ads can't ask for private details." |
| `NMLS_NUMBER_REQUIRED` | the frozen brand has no NMLS number | "Add your NMLS number in Brand." |
| `EQUAL_HOUSING_REQUIRED` | the disclosure line has no Equal Housing statement | "Add the Equal Housing line to your disclosure in Brand." |
| `RUN_DATES_INVALID` | the end date is not after today | "Choose an end date after today." |
| `LIBRARY_AD_RETIRED` | the ad was retired before the check ran (009c D4) | "Choose another ad. Your budget, dates and area are kept." |

**What is checked.** "Checked text" is the headline, the primary text, and every Brand text the ad prints or carries: name, title, company, disclosure line, and lead form wording. The claim rule, `WORDS_INVALID_CHARACTERS`, `WORDS_CO_BRAND`, and the `%`, `$`, and number-word parts of `WORDS_NUMBER` read all of it. The digit part of `WORDS_NUMBER` reads the headline, primary text, title, and lead form wording without exception. In the name, company, and disclosure line a digit is allowed only inside a **license reference**: a run of 4 to 12 digits (counting digits only; single spaces or hyphens may separate them), that directly follows "NMLS", "NMLS ID", "license", "lic", or "lic." (each optionally followed by "#" or ":"); a bare "#" is not a keyword (matched on the normalised text, case-insensitive). In the name and company fields only, an **ordinal** is also allowed: a token matching `\b\d{1,3}(st|nd|rd|th)\b`, case-insensitive ("1st", "21st"). A license reference or an ordinal is itself refused when it appears within two tokens, before or after, of any of: year, years, yr, yrs, month, months, mo, mos, monthly, payment, payments, pmt, percent, pct, down, fixed, arm, apr, rate, rates, points. Tokens are split on the normalised text at whitespace, punctuation, and every boundary between digits and letters, except that an ordinal keeps its suffix, so "#30yr" yields "30" and "yr". Every other digit in those three fields is refused, and the `%`, `$`, and number-word checks apply to them unchanged.

**The two `WORDS_CO_BRAND` exceptions, exactly.**

- **"Mortgage broker".** On the normalised text, the whole phrase "mortgage broker" delimited by non-letters on both sides (or the start or end of the text) is not a match for "broker". Every other term, including "brokerage", is matched against the original text first, and the phrase is never deleted before matching, so "mortgage brokerage", "mortgage brokers association", "broker partner", and "real estate broker" are still refused.
- **The NMLS host.** Every web address in the text is parsed as a URL (a bare host is read as `https://<host>`). It is allowed only when its host is exactly `nmlsconsumeraccess.org` or `www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org`, it is host-only (no path other than an empty one or a single "/", no query, and no fragment), it carries no user information and no port, and no other host appears anywhere in the same text, and the text is the disclosure line. Anything else is refused, including a subdomain or lookalike (`x.nmlsconsumeraccess.org.example.com`, `nmlsconsumeraccess.org.example.com`, `nmlsconsumer-access.org`), the host inside a path (`example.com/nmlsconsumeraccess.org`), user information (`nmlsconsumeraccess.org@example.com`), and the allowed host in the headline, primary text, title, company, or lead form wording. Before any rule runs, each text is NFKC-normalised and lower-cased, and the claim rule runs twice: on the normalised text, and on a copy with all whitespace and punctuation removed, so "3 . 5 %" and "r a t e s" are caught. Look-alike letters from other scripts are folded to Latin before matching. Refusing every digit, `%`, and `$` in the editable words is the conservative default until counsel approves a narrower list; "Down payment help" stays legal because it states no amount.

**The private-details list.** `WORDS_PRIVATE_INFO_REQUEST` reads all checked text, because Meta prohibits ads that directly request personal or certain financial information ([Meta rules check](research/2026-10-02-meta-special-ad-category-check.md), section 4). On the normalised text, after any run of two or more single letters separated by dots or spaces is joined ("S.S.N." and "s s n" read as "ssn"), it refuses any of these whole words or phrases, the words of a phrase separated by any run of whitespace or punctuation and an apostrophe optional: social security number, ssn, date of birth, birth date, birthdate, dob, driver's license, driver license, passport number, tax id, taxpayer id, itin number, account number, routing number, card number, cvv, security code, pin number, password, maiden name. A word that names a benefit or a product is not on the list, so "Social Security income" and "ITIN loans" pass, and words match only whole, so "Pin Oak Lending" passes. Whether wording that asks for a credit score or an income is a request for financial information is UNVERIFIED and for counsel; the list stays as it is until then.

The claim detector is deterministic (patterns, not a model). Whether the law requires the NMLS number on every mortgage ad is not asserted (UNVERIFIED; G7 counsel); the band prints it, so the check keeps it from printing empty.

### D6. Budget and dates (D-22)

Daily $25, "Starts: when you launch it", "Ends" 14 days out, and a total prefilled as daily times days ($350). All editable within the ruleset's bounds (`open-house-draft.ts:178-180`: $5 to $1,000 a day, at most $5,000 in total).

### D7. Launch is a separate, honest button (D-9)

In PRD-009 "Launch on Facebook" is always disabled. A pure function chooses its one sentence:

| Meta | Ad | Version | Launching for the workspace | Sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not connected | any | any | any | Meta isn't connected yet, so connect it in Settings to launch this ad. |
| Connected | retired | any | any | This ad was taken out of the library on <date>, so this campaign can't launch. |
| Connected | active | not approved | any | Approve this version first. |

In the product the Meta input is always "not connected", because nothing stores a Meta connection; the other rows are reached only in tests.

### D9. Targets (design section 6.4)

For a workspace owner who can approve, from Home:

- **First campaign:** 6 activations to approval (Choose an ad, Use this ad, Add, Save and check, Approve this version, Yes, approve) and 1 typed field (one city or state).
- **Every campaign after:** 5 activations and 0 typed fields, because the area is remembered.
- **0 checkboxes, 0 uploads, 0 overlay steps.**
- **Sign-up to approved in under 300 seconds** (009G-AC-008).

## Security notes

- The save trusts no client-supplied ad, version, or campaign reference: each is resolved from the catalog or under the session's tenant context.
- The edited words and the Brand text printed on the band or carried in the lead form are the only free text that reaches an ad. The deterministic checks read all of it, normalised, before any approval, and the request can carry none of the Brand text (009D-AC-024).
- The launch button cannot reach a provider: no route exists for it to call.
