# Desired Outcome

**Customer segment:** Qualified loan officers in the AutomatedLO community who operate a HighLevel location and run or intend to run Realtor-supported property campaigns.

**Outcome statement:** Qualified loan officers can make an informed purchase decision on the Operation Automated LO founding offer within 14 days after seeing a truthful working demonstration, exact scope, setup requirements, operating boundaries, and approved refund terms.

**Measurement signal:** Distinct eligible founder accounts complete the $500 payment for the same versioned offer. Demo registrations, attendance, applications, stated intent, and checkout starts are diagnostic signals, not successful outcomes.

**Baseline:** Zero paid founder accounts recorded against the versioned founding offer.

**Target:** At least 15 eligible, distinct founder accounts remain paid under the approved terms by the end of the two-week experiment.

**Cycle started:** 2026-07-20

**Status:** active, G8 remains BLOCKED until verified commercial evidence satisfies the target.

## Decision boundary

- 15 or more paid accounts: demand criterion is eligible for independent verification.
- 10 to 14 paid accounts: inconclusive for production authorization. Run a decision review and do not mark G8 PASS.
- Fewer than 10 paid accounts: reshape or stop after confirming the demo and follow-up protocol were executed as designed.
- Refunded, duplicated, ineligible, test, complimentary, or team-owned purchases do not count.
