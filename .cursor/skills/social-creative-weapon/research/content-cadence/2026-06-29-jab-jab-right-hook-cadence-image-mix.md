# Jab/jab/jab/right hook - the value-vs-ask cadence behind the image mix

## Summary
The image-cadence directive in the weapon (clean quote CARDS for value beats = jabs; PHOTO or gradient-text OVERLAY for announcement/urgency = right hooks) derives from Gary Vaynerchuk's "Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook" (2013). Jabs are value/relationship content thrown "as often as possible"; the right hook is the ask/CTA, thrown rarely (Gary's extreme framing: "every 6 months or so"). The 2026 caveat: the core principle (give value before asking) holds, but context and platform-native, mobile-adapted creative matter MORE than in 2013.

## Key quotations / statistics
- "The 'jabs' represent value-driven content, which builds a relationship with the audience, while the 'right hook' is the call to action that converts followers into customers."
- A jab should be "intriguing, engaging, and/or create some sort of emotional response," thrown "as often as possible."
- Right-hook frequency (Gary's framing): "you should only be throwing a right hook every 6 months or so."
- 2026 relevance: published 2013, "core principle remains valid ... context matters more than ever ... high-quality content that's perfectly adapted to specific social media platforms and mobile devices."
