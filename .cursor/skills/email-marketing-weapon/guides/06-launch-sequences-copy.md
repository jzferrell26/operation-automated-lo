## The launch cadence (warm broadcast, the 6-12 email runway)

- Sweet spot: 7-12 emails across a 7-14 day window.
- Send roughly every other day in the main launch, then back-load: 2-3 emails in the final 48 hours. Over 50% of buyers purchase at the last minute, so the back-load is deliberate.
- A simple event spine: Invite, then Reminder, then Starting now, then Thank you + replay.

### Webinar registration math (set client expectations)

- A single invitation captures 40-50% of total registrations at best.
- A follow-up to non-registrants with a DIFFERENT subject/angle captures another 20-30%.
- Send 2-3 invitations with varied angles: outcome-focused, speaker-focused, urgency-focused.

## The webinar email spine (per-email roles)

1. Invitation: topic, a compelling brief description, link to the registration landing page, one strong single CTA.
2. Day-before reminder: rekindle interest, confirm details.
3. One-hour-before reminder: prompt to wrap up and prep.
4. "We're live" / starting-now: sent at the exact start time. A direct one-link "we're live" email can lift live attendance 10-15%.
5. Post-webinar follow-up + replay: send the replay within 2-4 hours of ending. Same-day follow-ups get 2-3x the engagement of next-day.

Post-webinar/launch close: stack value, then objection-handling, then urgency/scarcity, then final-call (the 2-3 emails in the last 48 hours).

## Subject lines and preheaders

- Length: 30-50 characters, specific, clear about what is inside. Clarity beats cleverness.
- Subject + preheader = a two-line headline. The subject grabs; the preheader deepens the hook with context the subject could not fit. Write at least about 75 characters of preheader so it is not truncated or auto-filled with body text.
- Proven structures:
  - Status/time markers: "STARTING SOON, [topic]", "Starting NOW, [topic]", "See you tomorrow, [topic]".
  - Curiosity + date: "Something's coming Thursday (you'll want to clear your calendar)".
  - Outcome-framed invite: "You're invited: [specific outcome]".
- Reframe: nobody registers for webinars, they register for knowledge, skills, and results. Lead with the transformation; the word "webinar" is secondary.

Note: the proven subject structures above use commas and the bullet character where a dash might be tempting. Keep it that way; no em dashes in any subject or preheader.

## CTA discipline

One primary CTA per email. The reminder and live emails should be a single direct link. Repeat the one CTA rather than offering competing actions.

## Cold cadence

For cold outbound, pair the structure here with the cold-outreach warmup ramp and the cold link-hygiene rules: no URL shorteners, consistent tracking domain, and the per-inbox volume ceiling (`03-monitoring-warmup-hygiene.md`, `04-spam-filter-html.md`).

## Re-engagement / sunset copy

2-3 re-engagement emails over 4-6 weeks before suppression. Use click-based engagement (Apple Mail Privacy Protection makes opens unreliable), a clear "do you still want to hear from us?" ask, then suppress non-responders. The deliverability half (engagement signal, suppression step) is in `03-monitoring-warmup-hygiene.md`.
