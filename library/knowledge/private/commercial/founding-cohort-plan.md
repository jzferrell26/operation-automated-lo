# Founding Cohort Plan

## Commercial objective

Use the AutomatedLO community to validate the product and generate the requested $10,000 without creating a high-touch onboarding obligation.

## Founding offer

| Item | Offer |
| --- | --- |
| Price | $500 one time |
| Capacity | 20 founding accounts |
| Included period | 90 days |
| Continuation | $197 per month after 90 days |
| Onboarding | One live group setup workshop, recording, checklist, and office-hours thread |
| Included product | GHL connection, brand/compliance profile, one Open House Boost blueprint, page/PDF/creative generation, Meta launch flow, GHL routing, reporting |
| Not included | Individual funnel builds, custom automation, ad management service, custom compliance drafting, database cleanup, voice agent, database reactivation |

Twenty buyers at $500 produce $10,000. That is about 5.8 percent of a 343-member community.

## What must exist before the offer

The launch needs a working demonstration, not a full SaaS:

1. One real or representative property input
2. One co-branded public page
3. One generated PDF and ad creative set
4. One campaign approval screen
5. One Meta draft or sandbox launch walkthrough
6. One GHL lead-routing demonstration
7. One simple results dashboard mock or working read-only view
8. A clear founding scope and refund policy

The demo can use a concierge backend. The promise must clearly distinguish working automation from manual founding-cohort operations.

## Launch message

The launch should be framed around the business outcome:

> Give a Realtor one property campaign that actually connects the page, flyer, ad, follow-up, and pipeline inside the HighLevel account you already use.

Avoid leading with AI agents, 21 tools, or Marketplace architecture. Those are implementation details, not the buying reason.

## Two-week launch sequence

### Days 1 to 3: proof and waitlist

- Select one Open House Boost example.
- Produce the page, PDF, QR code, creative, and GHL lead path.
- Record a three to five minute end-to-end demo.
- Publish a short founding-cohort application with the exact scope.
- Invite the community to a live demonstration.

### Days 4 to 6: live demonstration

- Show the old workflow and the one-flow replacement.
- Generate the campaign live from one property.
- Show the approval and the lead arriving in GHL.
- Present the 20-seat founding offer.
- Collect payment at the event and for 48 hours after it.

### Days 7 to 10: proof-based follow-up

- Publish answers to the most common objections.
- Share the generated assets, exact setup requirements, and founder capacity.
- Contact engaged members individually only when they have asked a product question or started checkout.
- Do not turn the sale into custom consulting.

### Days 11 to 14: close and schedule cohort

- Close enrollment at 20 seats or the stated deadline.
- Send one pre-work checklist.
- Run one group setup workshop.
- Publish the cohort build and release calendar.

## Validation events

Track the funnel as events:

- Demo registration
- Demo attendance
- Checkout started
- Purchase completed
- GHL install completed
- Brand profile completed
- First campaign generated
- First campaign approved
- First campaign published
- First lead routed
- First appointment or application attributed
- 90-day continuation

## Go and no-go thresholds

| Decision point | Go | Reshape or stop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founding demand | 15 or more paid | Under 10 after a working demo and direct follow-up |
| Setup | 70 percent complete setup | Under 50 percent without heavy support |
| Launch | 50 percent publish in 14 days | Under 30 percent publish in 30 days |
| Support | Under 30 minutes per account per month | Over 60 minutes after month one |
| Continuation | 70 percent at $197 per month | Under 50 percent |

## Unit-economics guardrails

- Founding payments fund the vertical slice, not custom account work.
- Property, rate, equity, and listing data costs must be metered by campaign or active contact before they are added.
- Model and rendering costs need per-campaign budgets and hard caps.
- Meta spend is paid directly by the client's ad account.
- No ad-spend float, shared card, or Realtor reimbursement in the first release.
- Support is group-first, documentation-first, and exception-based.

## Expansion path

Only after Open House Boost meets activation and continuation gates:

1. Add New Listing Spotlight.
2. Add a Realtor partner campaign history and approval portal.
3. Add event and preapproval blueprints.
4. Add agency portfolio health and reselling.
5. Evaluate licensed home-value, equity, or refinance signals.
6. Evaluate Google Ads after the mortgage targeting and disclosure controls have proven stable on Meta.
