# Experiment: Founding Cohort Paid Demand

## Experiment Contract

**Gate:** G8 Demand

**Status:** READY FOR INPUTS. G8 remains BLOCKED.

**Assumption under test:** At least 15 eligible, distinct founder accounts will complete a $500 payment for the same versioned offer after seeing a truthful working demo.

**Archetype:** Concierge pricing and paid-demand experiment.

**Run window:** 14 calendar days from the approved launch timestamp.

## Preconditions

The experiment must not start until all items below are complete:

- The UI Foundation Raid has produced a working demo suitable for a founder walkthrough.
- Every demo surface labels automated, manual, synthetic, unavailable, and future behavior accurately.
- The versioned offer matches the approved $500 price, 20-account capacity, 90-day included period, continuation option, included scope, and exclusions.
- Product leadership approves exact refund, cancellation, delivery, and cohort-start language.
- The payment path is authorized, tested, and capable of reconciling completed, failed, duplicated, canceled, and refunded payments from a signature-verified provider event or authenticated API read-back. A browser success redirect is never payment evidence.
- The event date, timezone, registration path, payment URL, sender identity, reply path, physical address, and unsubscribe mechanism are approved.
- Email deliverability receives a separate go or no-go review before any campaign is scheduled.
- No participant record, payment data, credential, borrower information, or raw recording is committed to Git.

## Versioned Offer Facts

| Field | Experiment value |
| --- | --- |
| Price | $500 one time |
| Capacity | 20 eligible founder accounts |
| Included period | 90 days |
| Continuation | $197 per month after 90 days, subject to final approved terms |
| Demand gate | At least 15 eligible accounts remain paid |
| Product boundary | The demo may include synthetic or manual operations. Production provider behavior remains gated by G1 through G7. |

The final customer-facing refund and delivery terms require explicit product approval before the experiment begins. This plan does not invent those terms.

## Success Criteria

The criteria are fixed before launch:

- PASS candidate: At least 15 eligible, distinct founder accounts complete payment and remain paid under the approved terms at the decision timestamp.
- INCONCLUSIVE: 10 to 14 qualifying accounts. Do not authorize production. Conduct a documented decision review.
- INVALIDATED OR RESHAPE: Fewer than 10 qualifying accounts after the complete demo, application, checkout window, and approved direct follow-up run as designed.
- Capacity stop: Close enrollment at 20 qualifying paid accounts.

Only independent verification may change G8 from BLOCKED. Test, duplicated, complimentary, team-owned, ineligible, canceled, charged-back, or refunded payments do not count.

## Run Protocol

### Before Day 1

1. Freeze an offer version and demo version.
2. Approve the exact scope, exclusions, setup requirements, payment terms, refund terms, privacy notice, and support boundary.
3. Define eligibility and disqualification rules before reviewing applicants.
4. Configure the authorized application and payment paths outside Git.
5. Prepare the aggregate evidence record with no live participant data in the repository.

### Days 1 to 3: Proof and Registration

1. Publish the truthful working demo and exact founding scope.
2. Invite the eligible community to the live demonstration.
3. Record registrations, applications, and questions in the authorized system.
4. Do not count interest as demand success.

### Days 4 to 6: Demonstration and Checkout

1. Show the current workflow and the proposed one-flow replacement.
2. Show every synthetic, manual, unavailable, and gated state before presenting price.
3. Present the same versioned offer to every participant.
4. Open the approved checkout path and record completed payment only from a signature-verified provider event or authenticated API read-back, reconciled idempotently. Never count a browser success redirect.

### Days 7 to 10: Proof-Based Follow-Up

1. Publish answers to recurring questions without expanding the offer.
2. Follow up only through the approved warm-channel protocol.
3. Do not convert objections into custom consulting promises.
4. Record disqualification, cancellation, failure, and refund outcomes.

### Days 11 to 14: Close and Reconcile

1. Close at 20 qualifying accounts or the stated deadline.
2. Reconcile application, eligibility, payment, cancellation, refund, and duplicate status.
3. Produce aggregate counts and the decision packet.
4. Keep G8 BLOCKED until an independent reviewer verifies the evidence.

## Eligibility Contract

An account counts only when all approved eligibility fields are satisfied. At minimum, the applicant should confirm that the organization is a mortgage or lending operator, controls or can obtain an appropriate HighLevel location, understands that provider gates remain open, accepts the exact included and excluded scope, and is not using a test, team-owned, or duplicate identity.

Do not request provider credentials, borrower data, card data, or unnecessary regulated information in the application.
