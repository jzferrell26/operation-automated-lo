# Product Definition

## Product

**Name:** Operation Automated LO

**Category:** HighLevel Marketplace SaaS for mortgage loan officers

**Initial market:** Jonathan's AutomatedLO community, currently 343 members according to the internal portfolio factsheet reviewed for this research

**Initial wedge:** Realtor partner campaign execution

**First campaign:** Open House Boost

## The job to be done

A loan officer wants to become useful to Realtor partners without managing another disconnected marketing stack. Given one property, event, or approved offer, the loan officer needs a complete, co-branded campaign that can be reviewed, launched, followed up, and measured inside the HighLevel account the team already uses.

The product must reduce seven disconnected jobs to one guided flow:

1. Collect the property, partner, brand, offer, and disclosure inputs.
2. Generate the campaign page and supporting assets.
3. Validate brand and mortgage-marketing constraints.
4. Let the loan officer and required compliance reviewer approve a frozen version.
5. Create or publish the Meta campaign through HighLevel's connected ad account.
6. Route leads into HighLevel contacts, opportunities, tags, calendars, and existing workflows.
7. Report the campaign from spend through funded or closed outcome.

## Positioning

Operation Automated LO is not a generic AI employee platform, a replacement CRM, or a full mortgage operating system. It is the campaign execution layer that turns a loan officer's brand, Realtor relationships, HighLevel data, and approved campaign blueprints into measurable mortgage business.

The short promise is:

> Turn one property into a complete co-branded campaign inside HighLevel.

## Primary user and buyer

The initial user and buyer is an individual loan officer or small mortgage team operating from a HighLevel sub-account. The future distribution buyer can also be a HighLevel agency that installs and resells the application across mortgage sub-accounts.

The Realtor is a collaborator, not the first paid seat. Realtor access should be a narrow approval and lead-sharing experience, not a second CRM.

## First vertical slice: Open House Boost

### Inputs

- Loan officer brand and required disclosure profile
- Realtor identity, brand, contact details, and approval status
- Property address, facts, photos, description, open-house schedule, and listing permission attestation
- Campaign goal, geography, daily budget, duration, and approved call to action
- GHL location, calendar, pipeline, stage, tags, and optional workflow mapping
- Meta page, Instagram account, ad account, pixel, and lead-form selection from HighLevel

### Outputs

- Responsive co-branded single-property campaign page
- Print-ready PDF flyer or property packet
- QR code and trackable short link
- Meta ad copy and correctly sized creative
- GHL or Meta lead form mapping
- Email and SMS follow-up package routed into an existing approved workflow
- Campaign record with approver, version, budget, launch state, and audit history
- Dashboard for spend, leads, appointments, applications, and closed outcomes

### Completion event

The job is complete when the campaign is live in the client-owned Meta ad account, the lead path has passed an end-to-end test, the campaign has a named approver and immutable version, and results can be connected to a GHL opportunity.

## Product boundaries

### Build in the first release

- One GHL location per tenant installation
- Loan officer and Realtor co-brand profile
- Open House Boost blueprint
- External single-property page and PDF generation
- Meta connection discovery through HighLevel
- Draft, review, explicit publish, pause, and read-only reporting
- HighLevel contact and opportunity routing
- Immutable campaign versions and write audit log
- Basic portfolio metrics for internal Cuantico operations
- Switchable light and dark themes across the authenticated dashboard, with a system-preference default

### Defer

- Google and LinkedIn ad publishing
- Automatic listing discovery from MLS data
- Home-value, equity, refinance, or predictive-mover data without a licensed provider
- Custom audience or customer-match uploads
- Autonomous budget optimization
- Ad-spend rebilling or Realtor cost-sharing
- A generic design studio, video editor, podcast tool, e-signature system, LOS, CRM, or lender marketplace
- Automated voice and database reactivation, which remain Product 1

## Success measures

### Founding validation

- At least 15 paid founding customers at $500 before committing to the full public Marketplace build
- Target 20 customers, which produces $10,000 and represents about 5.8 percent of the 343-member AutomatedLO community

### Activation

- 70 percent complete GHL, brand, and campaign setup
- 50 percent publish a campaign within 14 days
- Median time from campaign start to approval under 30 minutes after initial account setup
- Under 30 minutes of human support per account per month

### Retention and value

- 70 percent of founding customers continue at $197 per month after the included 90-day period
- At least 60 percent of launched campaigns have a verified lead path
- At least 40 percent of active accounts can connect an appointment or application to a campaign within 90 days

## Kill and reshape criteria

- Fewer than 10 paid founding customers after a working demo and direct launch to the community
- More than 60 minutes of manual support per account per month after the first month
- Fewer than 30 percent of paid accounts launch a campaign within 30 days
- Required property data or ad integrations cannot be licensed or safely accessed at founding-cohort economics
- Lender compliance teams refuse the approval model or require company-specific controls that cannot be expressed as tenant configuration

If any of these occur, retain the asset compiler as an internal Cuantico service and do not fund the full Marketplace product.
