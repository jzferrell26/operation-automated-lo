### Qualification notes

The public service login must be independently restricted to the intended role. The disposable fixture's privileged migration identity does not prove that a production login cannot `RESET ROLE`, inherit private privileges or `SET ROLE` to app/admission/scheduler capabilities. No production login was provisioned or inspected here. This is infrastructure qualification, not a customer role decision.

The deferred cleanup trigger has the precise limit recorded by Security: an arbitrary SQL caller may use `SET CONSTRAINTS ALL IMMEDIATE` to fire cleanup early. This removes scope and causes safe refusal; after re-deferring constraints, context can reacquire another currently eligible published selection. It does not provide private/prepared/revoked access. Do not claim an unconditional same-selection lifetime for every possible constraint timing; controlled PostgreSQL qualification should cover early cleanup and safe reacquisition. The current application uses fixed SQL and does not change constraints.

Physical purge execution, effective runtime grants, database parser/type resolution, serialized races, clock-bound expiry, trigger cleanup and narrow-login role reset/selection must be demonstrated in actual PostgreSQL before release. The source scanner and injected adapter cases cannot establish those facts. No source blocker is waived by this report.
