## Candidate Hypothesis Backlog

The following statements come from the product thesis and founding-cohort plan, not from completed customer interviews. They are prompts for discovery, not OST opportunity nodes:

- Loan officers may experience the property page, flyer, ad, follow-up, and CRM pipeline as one job split across disconnected tools and people.
- Loan officers may avoid repeatable property campaigns because setup effort and compliance uncertainty exceed the expected value.
- Loan officers may value a single HighLevel-centered workflow more than another standalone marketing tool.
- Buyers may prefer a fixed founding offer with explicit boundaries over custom consulting or open-ended ad management.
- Buyers may be unwilling to pay before provider contracts, production automation, and Launch Ready verification exist.
