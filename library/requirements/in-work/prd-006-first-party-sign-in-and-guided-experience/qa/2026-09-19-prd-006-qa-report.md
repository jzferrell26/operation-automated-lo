## Summary

PRD-006 is complete against its plan with no Critical finding: 94 of the 100 criteria pass on the evidence and 6 are partial. The four owner requirements are all met and visibly so. Email and password sign-in works with a visible "Forgot your password?" link and a footer that says the sign-in is separate from HighLevel; the guided setup runs account creation to Done in 78.0 seconds against a 300 second ceiling; the user-language guard reads the syntax tree across six roots and every exclusion was traced to confirm none reaches a review-mode screen; and the design sign-off is genuinely signed from real screenshots, of which this audit spot-checked eight across four frames and both themes. Six Warnings are worth closing: an auth route, a migration and a definer function that no criterion or amendment authorises; a post-approval screen that contradicts itself; three named design states with no capture; four em dashes; the in-flight database gate; and the two Medium security findings that `FSG-008` forbids leaving open. Recommend merging and closing the Warnings as follow-ups, except `FSG-008`, which is a wording question for `library-guardian`.
