# 02 - Asset cadence: jab / jab / right hook

ACTION step 2: pick the asset per beat. Value/teaching beats get clean quote CARDS (the jabs); announcement/urgency/day-of "right hooks" (the asks) get a PHOTO or a gradient-text OVERLAY. Vary so the feed is not a content mill.

## The doctrine

The cadence derives from Gary Vaynerchuk's "Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook" (2013). Jabs are value/relationship content thrown "as often as possible"; the right hook is the ask/CTA, thrown rarely (`research/content-cadence/2026-06-29-jab-jab-right-hook-cadence-image-mix.md`). The 2026 caveat: the give-value-before-asking principle holds, but platform-native, mobile-adapted creative matters more than it did in 2013.

## The mapping (this is the asset-selection rule)

| Beat type | Asset | Tool | Register example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Value / teaching (jab) | Clean quote CARD | resvg (`guides/03-cards-resvg.md`) | Strategist serif card, or HF Bomb bold card |
| Announcement / event detail | Event CARD | resvg (`guides/03-cards-resvg.md`) | Strategist event card |
| Urgency / day-of / CTA (right hook) | PHOTO of the person, or gradient-text OVERLAY | sharp (`guides/04-photos-sharp.md`, `guides/05-overlays-sharp.md`) | photo with brand scrim + ask |

The contrast is the point. A wall of identical quote cards looks like a content mill. Mixing clean cards (jabs) with a human photo or a scrim overlay (right hooks) keeps the feed human.

## Keep the ratio skewed toward jabs

Gary's "right hook every 6 months" is hyperbole. The practical rule: asks are the minority of posts; most posts give value. Skew the posts file toward jabs so the cadence reads human. When you author `posts.json` (see `templates/posts.template.json`), tag each post with a `beat` and let the count of right-hook posts stay low.

## Vary the register too, not just the asset

Within the jabs, alternate registers when the brand has more than one (Strategist vs HF Bomb for Heather). Same asset type (card), different look, so even the value beats do not feel uniform. The register is selected from the spec field, never improvised.
